What the app does

Forecast and comparison support for wheat planning

GrainCast helps users compare wheat-growing locations using a short-term forecast and longer-term historical weather patterns.

Visualisations

What each section shows

The Map section shows all stations and highlights the currently selected primary and secondary comparison locations.

The Predictions section shows a 7-day outlook and recommendation for the selected primary location and wheat type.

The Historical Data section shows rainfall, wind speed, or temperature comparisons across the selected date range for the primary and secondary locations.
